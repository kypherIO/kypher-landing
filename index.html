      Bible Bot is a personal project by Rod Andrews, built to make Scripture a little easier to reach, read aloud, and actually live out during a busy week.

      Got an idea, a bug, or just want to say hi? Email Rod →

      Bible Bot runs entirely in your browser. Text: King James Version, World English Bible, and Berean Standard Bible — all public domain. Audio is generated locally via the Web Speech API — nothing is uploaded or stored.

      Study notes are brief, non-denominational starting points for reflection, not authoritative theological commentary — please study alongside your own church, pastor, or trusted resources.
