2\. Why the Fit v3 score makes the case stronger

A large market is only useful if one person can find the right accounts
inside it. That is what Fit v3 does. It weights the two things that
predict a closed logistics deal, a real freight budget and an existing
CoreTrust buying habit, at thirty percent each, and it keeps itself
honest about whether we can reach the account through a fifteen percent
actionability component. The rep does not read twenty five thousand
rows, they work the tier A and B accounts where the freight is real and
the door is open.

This matters to the decision directly. The score shows the actionability
component, whether we can reach a buyer, is currently the binding
constraint. Many high freight accounts under-score not because the
freight is not there, but because we do not yet hold a verified contact.
That is exactly the gap a data tool closes. The score does not just size
the market, it points at the specific thing to buy.

5\. What the money produces

We hold a verified contact for 879 strategic accounts today, before
spending a dollar on data, enough for one person to begin and prove the
motion. The economics model carries two scenarios. The current state,
one person with the agent researching the web, produces roughly fifteen
qualified opportunities a month at a cost per qualified opportunity that
is a small fraction of a single strategic account. The aspirational
state, with verified data and the connector and the private equity map,
lifts the actionability component, the reply rate, and the reach, and
produces several times that. In both, a single qualified strategic
account at one million dollars or more in freight is worth far more than
the entire monthly cost of the motion.

6\. The ask

Approve the Salesforce connector now, since the ability exists and only
an information security approval stands in the way. Activate the
ZoomInfo API on the seats we already own, because our own Fit v3 scoring
shows reachability is the binding constraint. Fund one private equity
focused enrichment platform, Grata preferred, Cyndx as the lower cost
alternative, because the ownership map is the highest leverage data we
can buy and our file proves it. Hold Apollo and premium automation for
later. We are not asking leadership to bet on a market that might be
there. We are asking to reach a market we can name and rank, one member
at a time.
